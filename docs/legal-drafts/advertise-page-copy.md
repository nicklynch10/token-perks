## Reach the people who actually read the pricing page

Token Perks is a deals-first publication for people who take AI spend seriously. Our readers compare providers line by line: **builders shipping on API credits, token-hackers stretching free tiers, and AI power users researching every dollar of monthly spend** before they commit.

They don't scroll past. They arrive with a purchase decision in progress — and they act on what they find: switching providers, upgrading plans, and telling their teams what they learned.

## Placements

**Newsletter sponsor slot.** One sponsor per issue, top of the email, in the sponsor's own words within our length limits. Your name is on it; we never dress an ad up as our editorial voice.

**Site placements.** A limited set of positions on token-perks.com, including the deals index and article pages. Sold per named placement, not per thousand impressions of wherever an ad server happens to land.

## How we work with advertisers

- **Clear labeling, always.** Every paid placement is tagged "Sponsored" and styled so no one mistakes it for our picks. A labeled ad readers trust outperforms a disguised one that burns the audience.
- **No injected verdicts.** You can buy placement; you cannot buy our conclusions. We don't change rankings, ratings, or deal picks for advertisers — that independence is why readers believe us, and why a placement next to our work is worth paying for.
- **We say no.** Products that don't fit our readers get turned down. Fewer, better placements, priced accordingly.
- **Honest reporting.** You get real performance numbers for your placement. We don't publish fabricated community metrics, and we don't report them to you either.
