Token Perks is a deals-first publication about AI pricing and plans. This policy explains what we collect — which, by design, is very little — and what we would collect if the site grows.

## What this site is today

Token Perks is a static website. Today we have:

- **No first-party accounts.** You don't sign in, and we don't ask who you are.
- **No first-party analytics or tracking.** We don't run cookies, pixels, fingerprinting, or session recording.
- **No behavioral advertising.** We don't build advertising profiles about you.

## Server logs

The site is served through AWS infrastructure (CloudFront and related services). Like nearly every website, those systems automatically record technical request logs, which can include:

- IP address
- Date, time, and requested URL
- Referrer and user-agent string

**Retention: a maximum of 90 days**, after which logs age out or are deleted. We use these logs for security, abuse prevention, and capacity planning — not to profile readers.

## If you contact us

When we add a contact form or mailer, we will ask only for **your name and email address**, and use them **only to reply to your message**. We won't add you to a mailing list without a separate, clear sign-up. [Confirm before launch.]

## Reader-submitted tips

If you submit a deal tip, we may publish a short quotation from it — capped at 280 characters, with a link to the original source — after review by two editors (see our Editorial and Commercial Policy). We publish your quote and link, not your identity, unless you ask us to credit you.

## Third-party transfers (today and later)

- **Today:** We don't sell or share personal information with third parties, and no advertising or affiliate networks run on the site.
- **Later, if we enable affiliate or partner links:** clicking certain outbound links may pass through a third-party affiliate or partner network, which is a transfer to that third party. Before we switch this on, we will update this policy and label each affected link adjacent to the link itself. You will always be able to tell.
- **Outbound links generally:** when you follow any link off our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.
