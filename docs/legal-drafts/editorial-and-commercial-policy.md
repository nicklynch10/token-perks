# Editorial and Commercial Policy

This page is our trust contract with readers: how we choose what to publish, what our badges mean, how we make money, and what we do when we get something wrong.

## How we pick deals

We publish deals we can verify against official sources — the provider's own pricing page, documentation, or an official announcement. For every deal we ask:

- Is there an **official source** stating the price, discount, or term?
- Does the deal apply to real buyers, with stated eligibility and dates?
- Can a reader act on it today?

If a deal can't clear that evidence bar, we don't publish it — or we publish it clearly marked as unverified. Every price carries a **`verified_at` date** (when a human last confirmed it against an official source), and we tell readers to re-verify before paying, because prices change.

## The Verified badge

A "Verified" badge means: **on the `verified_at` date, an editorial staff member checked this specific claim against an official source.** It means nothing else. It does not mean the price still holds today, that every plan variant was checked, or that the provider endorses us. Claims we couldn't check against an official source either don't get the badge or don't get published.

## Reader-submitted tips

Many leads come from readers — often finds posted on Reddit or similar communities. How we handle them:

- Submissions arrive manually; we don't scrape communities.
- We quote at most **280 characters**, always with attribution and a link to the original post.
- Every tip passes a **two-editor gate** before publication: one editor verifies the claim against official sources, a second approves the framing.
- We don't invent or inflate "community" signals. No fabricated vote counts, no made-up "readers report" numbers. If we can't source a number, we don't print it.

Original authors can request takedown at any time; we act within **7 days**.

## Artificial Analysis links

Where third-party pricing context is useful, we link to Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai) with attribution. **We link; we don't republish.** We never copy their data, tables, or charts onto our site. Current figures live on their page, not ours.

## How we make money

**Today:** Token Perks has **no active affiliate links**. Revenue comes from advertising and sponsorships.

**If we add affiliate links:** each one will carry an adjacent disclosure at the point of the link — not buried in a footer — and we'll update this policy and our privacy policy before the first one goes live.

**Sponsorships:** sponsored placements are clearly labeled ("Sponsored") and visually distinct from editorial content. Sponsors have **no role in editorial verdicts** — not on whether a deal is covered, what the verdict says, or where it ranks. Advertisers can buy our readers' attention; they cannot buy our conclusions.

## Corrections and takedown

We correct in the open. Corrections are **append-only**: we add a dated correction note rather than silently rewriting history, so readers can see what changed and when. (Routine price updates get a fresh `verified_at` entry, not a correction note.)
