SITE A — TASK 1: One simple plan recommendation

> I came here wanting one plan and one price, and this site at least *tries* to give me prices — but oh boy, the names lost me. "Moderato? Allegretto?" I'm buying for a writer, not a music student.

Recommendation in plain language: the only giftable-sounding option the site spotlights is the "Kimi K3 core membership." For a first gift I'd pick the cheapest tier, "Moderato" — $19/month, or about $15/month effective if prepaid annually (roughly $180 upfront for the year). The middle tier the site works through as its example is "Allegretto" — $39/month monthly, or about $31/month effective prepaid annually (about $372 upfront instead of $39 × 12 = $468). Higher tiers are $99/month (about $79 effective annual) and $199/month (about $159 effective annual).

Why it's safe-ish from surprise bills: the site frames these as flat subscriptions that simply "renew at $19–$199/mo" at the chosen tier, with no per-unit overage fee named — the catch is usage *caps*, not extra charges ("one shared credit pool" with "5-hour and weekly controls," so heavy use may pause rather than bill extra). Big caveat the site itself admits: "this site sells nothing" and I must "re-verify at official terms before paying" — so this is a pointer, not a checkout.

What confused me as a non-technical buyer: four musical tiers instead of one plan, plus talk of "$0.33/task at 120 tasks/mo" and "effective cost per task" — I don't know what a "task" is for my partner's writing, so I can't tell which tier is enough.

Gifting path: NONE SHOWN. No gift button, gift card, recipient flow, or annual gift prepay on the site itself. The site's own line is: "Gifting, where offered, is handled by the provider directly." Annual prepay exists at the provider level (that's the ~$15–$159/mo effective pricing), which *could* work as a one-and-done gift, but I'd have to figure that out on the provider's site myself.
