## One number

49 tasks a month — the break-even between Kimi's Allegretto tier and pay-as-you-go, at the site's illustrative reference. The math: Allegretto is $39 a month; the reference pay-as-you-go rate is $0.80 per task (a 100,000-token task at $8 per 1M blended tokens). $39 ÷ $0.80 ≈ 49. Below roughly 49 tasks a month, pay-as-you-go or a free route costs less; above it, the flat tier does. Annual prepay ($372 upfront, about $31 a month effective) moves the line to about 39. Detail: https://token-perks.com/best/kimi-k3-core/

## One caveat

The shared credit pool. Membership usage on Kimi K3 draws from a single balance, and 5-hour plus weekly controls can throttle burst sessions even when monthly credits remain — a long agent run can stall mid-day on a plan that looks paid up. A related routing note: tools must target the coding endpoint (api.kimi.com/coding/), not a pay-as-you-go key, or the usage does not draw from the membership at all.
