## One number worth having

At the site's illustrative reference — a 100,000-token task at $8 per 1M blended tokens — pay-as-you-go runs about $0.80 per task. Against that rate, the $39 Allegretto tier breaks even near 49 tasks a month; annual prepay drops the effective price to about $31 a month and the break-even to about 39. Below those lines, pay-as-you-go or a free route costs less. The worked example: https://token-perks.com/guides/monthly-vs-annual-ai/
