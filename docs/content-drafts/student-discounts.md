## "Will I be charged later?" — the question behind the query

Every "free for students" AI offer is a **timed subscription**, not a gift. Three renewal mechanics you'll meet across the table above:

1. **Re-verification gates** (GitHub, Windsurf, Google): free only while you can prove enrollment again. Miss the re-verify email and the meter starts — Windsurf's version converts to paid billing after the lapse or the 4-year cap.
2. **Hard end dates** (ChatGPT Plus 4-month; OpenAI teachers Jun 2027): the countdown starts at claim, not at graduation. The Oct 31 2026 claim deadline means waiting costs you the offer entirely.
3. **Auto-bill conversion** (all of them): a payment method is on file from day one in most flows. The charge lands unless you cancel first — the same continued-use-equals-agreement mechanic that governs paid renewals (see [what renewal actually does per vendor](/cursor-annual-renewal/)).

Set the reminder **when you claim**, not when the charge arrives.
