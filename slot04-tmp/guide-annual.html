Monthly vs annual AI plans

Short answer: stay monthly until your volume clears break-even for several consecutive months, then prepay annually. Annual Kimi K3 billing saves ~20% — Allegretto at ~$31/mo effective (~$372 upfront) vs $39/mo monthly — but only pays if you clear break-even (~39 tasks/mo) all year. Figures verified Sep 6 2026.

Worked example: Allegretto

  1. Monthly path: $39 × 12 = $468/year, cancellable any month.
  2. Annual path: ~$31 × 12 ≈ $372 upfront, saving roughly $96/year (~20.5%).
  3. Break-even moves with the price: ~49 tasks/mo monthly vs ~39 annual (against the $0.80/task reference).
  4. Risk check: if usage collapses in month 4, the monthly user has spent $156; the annual user has spent $372 minus whatever refund the terms allow — read them first.

When annual applies

  • Volume above break-even for 3+ consecutive months with no seasonal dip.
  • Refund / cancellation terms read and acceptable.
  • Caps (5-hour/weekly) already proven against your heaviest week.

When annual does not apply

  • New team, new tool, unmeasured usage — measure quarterly first.
  • Dependence on a limited-time promo (the Zen free route, or any similar provider promo window) that could end.
  • Cash-flow matters more than a 20% discount in the short run.

Prepay checklist

  • Refund window and proration, in writing, from the official terms.
  • Who owns the seat if a teammate leaves mid-year.
  • Calendar reminder 30 days before the annual renewal.

FAQ

How much does annual billing save on Kimi K3?

About 20%: Allegretto drops from $39/mo to ~$31/mo effective (~$372 upfront vs $468 over a year), saving roughly $96/year. Moderato, Allegro, and Vivace scale similarly ($19→$15, $99→$79, $199→$159). Verified Sep 6 2026.

When should I pay annually?

When your volume has cleared break-even for 3+ straight months (about 39 tasks/mo on Allegretto annual vs ~49 on monthly, against the $0.80/task reference) and you have confirmed the refund and cancellation terms.

When should I stay monthly?

Seasonal or experimental usage, teams still measuring volume, anyone relying on a free promo that could end, and any plan whose refund terms you have not read.

Does annual change the limits?

No — the shared credit pool plus 5-hour and weekly controls apply either way. Annual only locks the price; it does not raise the caps.

When does annual billing pay back vs monthly (break-even month)?

Annual is $372 upfront; monthly is $39 a month. If you cancel early with no refund, monthly is cheaper until about month 10 (372 ÷ 39 ≈ 9.5). Stay past month 10 and annual reaches break-even, saving roughly $8/mo thereafter. A full-refund window would move break-even to month 1 — which is why the refund terms decide the answer.

What happens on cancel — refunds, proration, keep-until-end-of-term?

Monthly is the low-risk path: cancel and access runs to the end of the paid month, then stops — no refund needed. Annual keeps you on the tier until the term ends, but refund and proration rules are the provider's to set and are not recorded in our Sep 6 snapshot. Confirm both in the official terms before prepaying $372.
