Membership vs PAYG: break-even Think in effective cost per task: monthly price divided by tasks you actually complete. Allegretto at $39 over 120 tasks is about $0.33 per task — less than half the $0.80 illustrative PAYG reference for a 100k-token task. The break-even against the $0.80/task reference is about 49 tasks a month on Allegretto ($39 ÷ $0.80) and about 24 tasks on Moderato ($19 ÷ $0.80). Below those lines, PAYG or a free tier is cheaper. Annual billing drops Allegretto to about $31/mo effective (~$372 upfront), which moves break-even down to roughly 39 tasks a month — worth it only if your volume is stable year-round. The shared pool has a throughput cost too: if 5-hour or weekly controls idle you for half a day each week, discount the membership's value by that lost throughput before comparing. How does the shared credit pool restrict usage? Membership usage draws from one shared credit pool, and 5-hour plus weekly controls can throttle burst usage even when monthly balance remains. Spread heavy agent runs across the week instead of one marathon day. Coding endpoint or PAYG — which do I use? Use the coding endpoint at https://api.kimi.com/coding/ for membership billing. A PAYG API key bills separately, so pointing tools at the wrong route means the membership does not cover that usage. When is annual billing worth it? When your monthly volume is stable and clears break-even (about 39 tasks/mo on Allegretto annual vs ~49 on monthly, against the $0.80/task reference). If your usage is seasonal, stay monthly until the pattern is proven. Is API credit use cheaper than a $20 subscription, and at what usage? At the $0.80/task reference, $19 of pay-as-you-go buys about 24 tasks — below that, credits or a free route are cheaper; above it, the $19 Moderato month is cheaper. The routes do not mix: a PAYG key bills separately from the coding-endpoint membership, so check which one your tools actually hit. How current is this information?
