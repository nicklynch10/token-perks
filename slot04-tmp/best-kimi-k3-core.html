Caveats

  • One shared credit pool: membership usage draws from a single balance, so parallel projects and long agent runs drain it together
  • 5-hour and weekly controls: burst caps can throttle you mid-day even when the monthly balance looks healthy
  • Tier jump is steep: Allegretto ($39) to Allegro ($99) is a 2.5x step — usage between those tiers is priced comparatively poorly
  • Annual discount is upfront cash: the ~20% effective saving requires a full-year prepay with terms you must confirm first

Summary

Pick Kimi K3 membership if you run steady agentic coding volume and want one predictable bill. At Allegretto ($39/mo, about $31/mo effective annually) it beats pay-as-you-go once you clear roughly 40–50 heavy tasks a month — below that, PAYG or a free route costs less. The constraint: a single shared credit pool plus 5-hour and weekly controls means marathon sessions can stall mid-sprint.

Membership vs PAYG: break-even

Think in effective cost per task: monthly price divided by tasks you actually complete. Allegretto at $39 over 120 tasks is about $0.33 per task — less than half the $0.80 illustrative PAYG reference for a 100k-token task.

The break-even against the $0.80/task reference is about 49 tasks a month on Allegretto ($39 ÷ $0.80) and about 24 tasks on Moderato ($19 ÷ $0.80). Below those lines, PAYG or a free tier is cheaper.

Annual billing drops Allegretto to about $31/mo effective (~$372 upfront), which moves break-even down to roughly 39 tasks a month — worth it only if your volume is stable year-round.

The shared pool has a throughput cost too: if 5-hour or weekly controls idle you for half a day each week, discount the membership's value by that lost throughput before comparing.

FAQ

What does Kimi K3 membership cost as of the Sep 2026 snapshot?

Moderato $19, Allegretto $39, Allegro $99, and Vivace $199 per month. Annual billing lowers the effective monthly cost to about $15 / $31 / $79 / $159, paid upfront. Prices verified in the Sep 6 2026 snapshot — re-check the official page before paying.

How does the shared credit pool restrict usage?

Membership usage draws from one shared credit pool, and 5-hour plus weekly controls can throttle burst usage even when monthly balance remains. Spread heavy agent runs across the week instead of one marathon day.

Coding endpoint or PAYG — which do I use?

Use the coding endpoint at https://api.kimi.com/coding/ for membership billing. A PAYG API key bills separately, so pointing tools at the wrong route means the membership does not cover that usage.

When is annual billing worth it?

When your monthly volume is stable and clears break-even (about 39 tasks/mo on Allegretto annual vs ~49 on monthly, against the $0.80/task reference). If your usage is seasonal, stay monthly until the pattern is proven.

Is API credit use cheaper than a $20 subscription, and at what usage?

At the $0.80/task reference, $19 of pay-as-you-go buys about 24 tasks — below that, credits or a free route are cheaper; above it, the $19 Moderato month is cheaper. The routes do not mix: a PAYG key bills separately from the coding-endpoint membership, so check which one your tools actually hit.
