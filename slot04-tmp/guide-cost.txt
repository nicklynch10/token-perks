Figures verified Sep 6 2026. Worked example: the 50-task break-even Subscription side: $40 ÷ 120 tasks ≈ $0.33/task. Fixed price, so every extra task makes the average cheaper. PAYG side: 120 tasks × $0.80 = $96. Linear price — task 1 and task 120 cost the same. Break-even: $40 ÷ $0.80 = 50 tasks. Above 50, subscribe; below 50, pay per task. Note: the $0.80 reference assumes 100k tokens/task (median, trailing 7-day example window). Heavy agent runs can double it — which halves break-even to 25.
