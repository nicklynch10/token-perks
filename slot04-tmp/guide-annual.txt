When annual applies Volume above break-even for 3+ consecutive months with no seasonal dip. Refund / cancellation terms read and acceptable. Caps (5-hour/weekly) already proven against your heaviest week. When annual does not apply New team, new tool, unmeasured usage — measure quarterly first. Dependence on a limited-time promo (the Zen free route, or any similar provider promo window) that could end. Cash-flow matters more than a 20% discount in the short run. Prepay checklist Refund window and proration, in writing, from the official terms. Who owns the seat if a teammate leaves mid-year. Calendar reminder 30 days before the annual renewal. FAQ How much does annual billing save on Kimi K3? About 20%: Allegretto drops from $39/mo to ~$31/mo effective (~$372 upfront vs $468 over a year), saving roughly $96/year. Moderato, Allegro, and Vivace scale similarly ($19→$15, $99→$79, $199→$159). Verified Sep 6 2026. When should I pay annually? When your volume has cleared break-even for 3+ straight months (about 39 tasks/mo on Allegretto annual vs ~49 on monthly, against the $0.80/task reference) and you have confirmed the refund and cancellation terms. When should I stay monthly? Seasonal or experimental usage, teams still measuring volume, anyone relying on a free promo that could end, and any plan whose refund terms you have not read. Does annual change the limits? No — the shared credit pool plus 5-hour and weekly controls apply either way. Annual only locks the price; it does not raise the caps. When does annual billing pay back vs monthly (break-even month)? Annual is $372 upfront; monthly is $39 a month. If you cancel early with no refund, monthly is cheaper until about month 10 (372 ÷ 39 ≈ 9.5). Stay past month 10 and annual reaches break-even, saving roughly $8/mo thereafter. A full-refund window would move break-even to month 1 — which is why the refund terms decide the answer. What happens on cancel — refunds, proration, keep-until-end-of-term? Monthly is the low-risk path: cancel and access runs to the end of the paid month, then stops — no refund needed. Annual keeps you on the tier until the term ends, but refund and proration rules are the provider's to set and are not recorded in our Sep 6 snapshot.
